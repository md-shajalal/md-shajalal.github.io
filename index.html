I am a postdoctoral researcher in IT Security and Consumer Computing at the University of Siegen, Germany. My current work focuses on human-centered explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) across several domains, including audio deepfake detection, smart homes and natural language processing.

My PhD thesis, Towards Human-Centered Actionable Explainable AI-enabled Systems, was awarded Summa Cum Laude (highest distinction) at the University of Siegen. I carried out this research as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow (MSCA ITN) at Fraunhofer FIT, Germany.

Before joining Fraunhofer FIT, I was a faculty member in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at HSTU, Bangladesh. I completed my master’s degree in Computer Science at Toyohashi University of Technology, Japan, under the MEXT scholarship, with a thesis on identifying diverse query intents for ambiguous and under-specified search queries. I have also taught at Bangladesh Agricultural University and Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology, and worked for a year as a software engineer.

My research interests lie in explainable AI, information retrieval and natural language processing.

                        Dataset

                        Bengali Semantic Textual Similarity (STS) dataset

                        Measuring textual similarity is central to many retrieval and NLP applications, including summarization, machine translation, plagiarism detection, sentiment analysis, relevance feedback and subtopic mining.

                        Task. Given two Bengali sentences, a method returns a continuous similarity score from 0 to 5, where 0 means the sentences are semantically independent and 5 means they are equivalent. Performance is measured by the Pearson correlation between system scores and human judgments.

                        Details are in our paper Semantic Textual Similarity in Bengali Text (IEEE ICBSLP 2018). If you use this dataset, please cite it.
